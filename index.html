Little ways to dress up a vanity

Start with one corner. Set out a small tray for the jewellery you wear most, tuck your daily makeup into a pouch, and leave a little clear room for getting ready.

A pink ribbon around a brush cup or a tiny dish for your rings can make the whole spot feel more like you.

Three things I look for in a cute accessory

Can I wear it with clothes I already own? Will it be comfortable all day? Would I still like it if it came in a different colour?
